# Phase 9: Soundtrack (about 16 SP)

> [Planning index](../README.md) · [Master plan](../Master/master_plan.md) · [← Phase 8](phase_8_hands_on_governance.md)

**Phase goal:** music that follows the presidency. The game so far plays one nine-second ambient pad on repeat, the same on the menu, in peacetime and at war. Phase 9 replaces it with an adaptive soundtrack: calm while the country is calm, tense when it is not, heavy in wartime, building as election day nears, with short musical cues for the big moments.
**Milestone:** **M9: Soundtrack**

## 1. Decisions

| Question                    | Decision                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recorded tracks or code?    | **Code.** Every note is synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API, as the sound effects already are. No audio files, no licences    |
| Generated by an AI service? | **No.** The soundtrack is composed by rules in `src/ui/audio/music/`                                                                    |
| Style                       | A calm strategy-game score with an Indian classical colour: drone, plucked strings, bamboo flute, hand drums                            |
| Signature phrases           | **None.** Melodies come from scales and simple rules; no raga's characteristic phrases, no known songs, nothing from the reference game |
| Does music touch the sim?   | **Never.** The mood is read from the game state in the UI; the music has its own seeded generator and does not use the game's `Rng`     |
| Does it change saves?       | **No.** The music style is a player setting (`platform/settings`), not part of `GameState`                                              |

## 2. Musical design

### Instruments (all synthesised)

| Instrument | How                                                                                           | Role                                            |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Drone      | Root, fifth and octave as detuned saw waves through a low-pass filter, with a slow swell      | The ground every mood sits on                   |
| Pluck      | Karplus–Strong string (a noise burst into a tuned feedback delay), with a short bright attack | Melody in calm moods                            |
| Flute      | Sine wave plus band-passed breath noise, gentle vibrato and slides between notes              | Melody on the menu and in the election build-up |
| Hand drums | A low pitched-down sine thump and a high tuned tap, plus a noise slap                         | Rhythm                                          |
| Pad        | Soft triangle chord with a slow attack                                                        | Harmony under tension and war                   |

One shared delay-based reverb sits on the music bus; the bus has a voice cap so a long session never piles up nodes.

### Moods

| Mood       | When                                                             | Mode (scale)             | Tempo | Character                                                     |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Menu       | Main menu, New Game, game over                                   | Major pentatonic         | 60    | Flute over drone, no drums, ceremonial                        |
| Governance | Normal play                                                      | Major (Ionian-like)      | 76    | Plucked melody, light drums in a cycle of 8                   |
| Tension    | Hottest region at the riot trigger, or approval near impeachment | Minor with a flat second | 70    | Low drone, pad, sparse insistent drums in a cycle of 7        |
| War        | Any active war                                                   | Minor (Aeolian-like)     | 92    | Heavy low drums every beat, pad, no bright plucks             |
| Election   | The last six months before election day (not in endless mode)    | Mixolydian-like          | 84    | Flute and pluck together, drums that thicken as the day nears |

Priority when several apply: war, then tension, then election, then governance. Moods change only at the end of a bar and crossfade over a few seconds. To stop the music flapping when a number hovers on a threshold, a mood must hold for a few seconds before it switches.

### Stingers

Short cues (4–8 seconds) that duck the music: **victory**, **defeat**, **war declared**, **war ended**. They replace the `success` and `failure` chimes at the end of a game.

## 3. Architecture

- `src/ui/audio/music/theory.ts`: scales, note-to-frequency, the seeded generator (mulberry32) used only by the music.
- `src/ui/audio/music/moods.ts`: the mood table above (scale, tempo, cycle, layers).
- `src/ui/audio/music/composer.ts`: pure. Given a mood, a bar number and a generator, returns the bar's notes (instrument, time in beats, pitch, length, level). Testable without audio.
- `src/ui/audio/music/mood.ts`: pure. `moodFor(screen, game)` reads the game through the `src/sim` index and returns a mood.
- `src/ui/audio/music/instruments.ts`: turns a note into Web Audio nodes.
- `src/ui/audio/music/player.ts`: a look-ahead scheduler on the audio clock (replaces the drifting `setTimeout` loop), the crossfade, the voice cap and the stingers.
- `useAudio.ts` works out the mood from `gameStore` and passes it on; the music pauses while the page is hidden.
- Settings: **Music style**: _Adaptive_ (default), _Calm only_ (always menu or governance), _Off_.
