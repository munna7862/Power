## India: Apex Sovereign 1.0.0

You are the President of India. Five years, every rupee, minister, crisis, treaty and soldier, and then the voters decide.

This is the first public release: a complete country-simulation game with no ads, no in-app purchases and no paid shortcuts.

### What's in the game

- **Run the country:** an economy of five sectors, taxes and a monthly budget, eleven ministries and a cabinet of fictional ministers, ideology and development strategy.
- **Survive the job:** disasters, scandals, protests, terror plots, coups and assassination attempts; more than 140 events, each one rarely repeated.
- **Command the forces:** twelve kinds of units, research, drills, espionage, wars on terrain-named fronts and a nuclear programme you hope never to use.
- **Lead on the world stage:** a world map of 205 countries with India's boundaries as per the Survey of India, trade contracts, non-aggression pacts, embassies, aid and sanctions, and twenty AI nations with their own agendas.
- **Win a second term:** reach 55% approval on election day as voter fatigue builds, then stay on in endless mode if you like.
- **Your way:** Easy, Normal and Hard; optional no-wars and no-riots modes; a guided tutorial; the "Constitution" handbook explaining every number; full keyboard play; interface sizes 90–150%; a colour-blind-safe palette; reduced motion; music and sound you can tune.

| System  | File                                                                                   |

| Windows | `India-Apex-Sovereign-Setup-1.0.0.exe` (installer) or `…-1.0.0-win-x64.zip` (portable) |

| Linux   | `…-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `.tar.gz`                                           |

The builds are not code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run ("More info" → "Run anyway"); on macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.

Saves are kept in your user folder and survive updates and uninstalls. Autosaves are written safely and the last three are kept.

### Known issues

- Simulation text (event stories, inbox reports and "Why?" explanations) is English only; the interface itself is ready for translation, and Hindi is planned.
- The balance targets a strong player; feedback on how Normal feels is very welcome.
