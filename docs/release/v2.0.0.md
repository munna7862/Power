## India: Apex Sovereign 2.0.0: Hands-on Governance

You are the President of India. Five years, every rupee, school, power plant, factory, crisis, treaty and soldier, and then the voters decide.

This release puts the country in your hands. There are no ministers any more: you run eleven departments yourself, build what the country needs, keep the lights on, trade what it makes and buy what it defends itself with. No ads, no in-app purchases, no paid shortcuts.

### What's new

- **Departments, not ministers:** eleven departments whose services you rate up from 1 to 5 stars with treasury money. Starve one for six months and it loses a star.
- **Build the country:** schools, universities, clinics, hospitals, water, housing and transport in every region, ready in weeks or months.
- **Police & Internal Affairs:** recruit officers, build stations, raise special units and fund agencies to bring down crime, unrest and terror, without trampling civil liberties.
- **Power grid:** solar, wind, coal, gas, hydro and nuclear plants. Fall short and the power cuts hit growth and approval; burn too much coal and the air pollution does too.
- **Industry and trade:** 18 kinds of plants turn 15 goods into goods. Run them yourself or license them to private investors, then sell the surplus to partners or the world market, sign supply contracts and set tariffs.
- **The Armoury:** buy the forces from one screen; deliveries take days to weeks.
- **Money by the day:** see the treasury's daily change, take loans from friendly countries or an international lender, repay public debt early, and let the advisor point at the most urgent problem.
- **New options:** turn off **Regional play** to run one India without region pickers, or turn on **Calm months** for at most one random event a month.

Re-election now needs 58% approval on Normal and Hard (55% on Easy). The game also fixes the ten defects found in the 1.0 QA review.

### Saves

Saves from 1.0 cannot be continued: they ran the country through a cabinet of ministers. Loading one explains this and asks you to start a new game. Saves from this version on keep loading in later versions.

### Known issues

- Simulation text (event stories, inbox reports and "Why?" explanations) is English only; the interface itself is ready for translation, and Hindi is planned.
- The balance targets a strong player; feedback on how Normal feels is very welcome.
